## Research

### Healthcare Knowledge Representation

**Knowledge Representation for Healthcare Systems: A Comparative Analysis of Performance of Different Representation Paradigms**

A comparative study of seven knowledge-representation paradigms for healthcare question answering across increasing levels of reasoning complexity.

The work involved a benchmark of **47 cardiovascular patient records and five clinical queries**, with representation paradigms evaluated using **query coverage, reasoning depth, and explainability**. Knowledge Graphs achieved complete benchmark coverage while supporting multi-hop and contextual reasoning.

Published in *Intelligent Computing: Proceedings of the 2026 Computing Conference*, Springer, 2026.

**DOI:** [10.1007/978-3-032-24807-7_4](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-24807-7_4)

### Geopolitical Risk & High-Frequency Financial Markets

**Cryptocurrency Markets and Geopolitical Risk: A Study of the Israel–Gaza Conflict**

Ongoing collaborative research examining the response of cryptocurrency and traditional financial markets to geopolitical shocks using **time-stamped news and hourly financial data**.

The study involves constructing an **Hourly War Risk Index (HWRI)** to characterize escalation and de-escalation shocks and applying high-frequency event-study methods using **Abnormal Returns (AR), Cumulative Abnormal Returns (CAR), and Cumulative Average Abnormal Returns (CAAR)** across multiple event windows.

The broader analysis combines **news-based risk measurement, time-series analysis, statistical learning, statistical inference, and financial econometrics**.
